About the Artist

Sania Anees

Master Calligrapher & Islamic Art Specialist

With over 8 years of dedicated practice in traditional Islamic calligraphy, I specialize in transforming sacred verses into breathtaking visual art. My journey began under the guidance of renowned masters in Lahore, where I mastered various scripts including:

  • Thuluth Script
  • Naskh Script
  • Diwani Style
  • Kufic Geometrics

Each artwork is created with traditional tools - bamboo pens, handmade inks, and archival-quality paper - ensuring authenticity and longevity. My work has been featured in exhibitions across Pakistan and collected by art enthusiasts worldwide.
